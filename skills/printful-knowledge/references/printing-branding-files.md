# Printing, branding, and files

## Printing methods

Printful can support multiple production methods depending on the product and placement, including methods such as DTG, DTF/DTFlex, embroidery, sublimation, direct-to-fabric, cut-and-sew, and others.

Do not assume every product, variant, region, or placement supports the same methods.

## Placements and branding

Placements and branding options are product-specific and can change.

Do not assume:
- every product supports every placement;
- inside or outside labels are universal;
- the same production method applies to every placement;
- an extra placement is included in a base price.

Verify the exact product/variant requirements when production or margin decisions depend on them.

## Print files

Treat exact print-file dimensions, DPI requirements, file limits, and placement specifications as current product-specific facts.

Do not invent technical dimensions. Use current official Printful guidance for the exact product/placement.

A mockup is not a production master. Do not derive final print artwork from a screenshot or mockup image.
