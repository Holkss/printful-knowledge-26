# Catalog and variants

## General model

Use products for model-level reasoning and variants for exact size/color or otherwise distinct physical options.

## Availability

Catalog presence is not the same as current availability.

When availability matters, scope the claim to:
- the exact variant when possible;
- the relevant region;
- the relevant printing technique or fulfillment context;
- the date/time of verification.

Use `unknown` when a source does not establish availability.

## Region

Do not generalize availability from one selling or fulfillment region to another.

## Dynamic catalog facts

Verify current official information when the task depends on:
- current product lineup;
- current variant IDs/status;
- color and size availability;
- regional availability;
- supported printing techniques;
- placements/branding options;
- current catalog/base pricing.
