# Pricing, shipping, and fulfillment

## Pricing

Separate, when applicable:
- base/catalog item cost;
- print or placement charges;
- branding/additional placement charges;
- account or volume discounts;
- taxes/VAT;
- shipping;
- final order cost.

A catalog/base price is not automatically the final configured production cost.

## Shipping

Shipping can depend on destination, products, quantities, fulfillment options, current service availability, and store/order configuration.

Treat exact shipping rates and delivery estimates as dynamic.

## Fulfillment

Fulfillment availability and timing are operational facts. Verify them when a user's decision depends on current status.

## Margin work

When calculating a margin, state which cost components are included and which are still unknown/current.
